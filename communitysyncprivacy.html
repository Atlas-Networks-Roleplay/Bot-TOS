Atlas Networks Community Sync Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 05/04/2025 • Last Updated: 05/04/2025

1. Information Collected

Atlas Networks Community Sync may collect the following non-personal information during use:

  • Command usage (e.g., what commands are used and when)
  • Error logs and system messages for debugging purposes
  • Server IDs, User IDs, or Message IDs used solely for bot functionality

No personal, sensitive, or identifiable data (e.g., names, emails, IP addresses) is collected unless explicitly stated.

2. How We Use Data

  • To operate and maintain bot functionality
  • To monitor performance and resolve issues
  • To improve features and user experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any collected data with third parties. Access to data is restricted to the development team and used only for administrative purposes.

4. Data Retention

Collected data is retained only as long as necessary for functionality and maintenance. Logs may be periodically cleared or anonymized.

5. Your Rights

If you are concerned about any data collected or would like it removed, please contact the developers through the official support server or contact method listed in the bot’s profile or documentation.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the bot after changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
