Privacy Policy – Atlas Networks FiveM Server

Effective Date: May 3, 2025 • Last Updated: May 3, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlas Networks collects, uses, and safeguards player data on our FiveM server. Your continued use of our platform indicates acceptance of these practices.

2. External Privacy Policies

As a FiveM-based server, usage of our services also implies agreement with the following policies:

  • Rockstar Games Privacy Policy
  • Cfx.re (FiveM) Privacy Policy

3. Data We Collect

  • In-game activity logs (kills, arrests, chat, actions)
  • Identifiers such as Steam, Rockstar, or FiveM IDs
  • Connection timestamps and server behavior metadata
  • Data pulled from associated Discord servers (e.g., roles, user IDs)

This data is used to support gameplay, moderation, and internal administrative functions.

4. Storage and Retention

Data may be stored securely on our systems for operational needs. It is not sold, published, or shared outside the Atlas Networks staff.

5. Security and Enforcement

We take steps to secure data and protect user privacy. Any attempt to compromise our server, website, Discord bots, or backend systems is strictly forbidden and will result in immediate and permanent bans.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be modified at any time. Continued use of the server constitutes acceptance of the current version.
